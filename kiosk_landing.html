Three steps to your destination.

Simple enough for a first-year student, clear enough for a visitor on day one.

  1. 01

    Search at the kiosk

    Type a building, office, or department and pick it from the results.

  2. 02

    Scan the QR code

    Open the code with your phone camera to carry your route with you.

  3. 03

    Follow the arrows

    Walk with AR arrows, distance, and voice guidance until you arrive.
